About

As the name says, NicheSite is a website with a collection of my niche, hobby-side projects and articles. The topics I focus on are either super niche that nobody bothers to give an f on or something I personally find interesting.

Why making this website?

I would like to call it a "public educational diary." While I am sharing topics that I am interested in, I ensure that they are helpful for some people. Additionally, as a (noob) self-taught web developer, I want to find out how well I make a real-world website after a good amount of time practicing and learning from YouTube and other resources. I am also hyped to make an environmentally friendly website thanks to the idea of Lowtech Magazine, and it would be super cool if I could achieve that with my own website.

How did I make this website?

  • No external CSS frameworks. Just pure CSS configurations, HTML, and a very little JavaScript, building from scratch. (Should I call that the most boomer way to make a website in 2026?)
  • Managed by 2 Python scripts to add and delete pages.
